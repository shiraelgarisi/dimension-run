HOW TO PLAY

Move left and right

Swipe ← → (or press ← → / A D) to change lanes and dodge blocks.

Jump

Swipe ↑ (or press ↑ / W) to jump over logs, spikes and holes.

Slide

Swipe ↓ (or press ↓ / S) to slide under arches and beams.

Switch worlds

Tap the screen (or press SPACE) to jump between the Light and Dark worlds. A wall in one world can be open in the other!

See-through shapes

Faint shapes are obstacles in the OTHER world. Check them before you switch!

Coins and the shop

Coins are saved. Spend them to keep going after a crash, or buy a 🛡️ shield or 🧲 magnet in the SHOP.

Diamonds and pets

Diamonds hide in chests and off the safe path. Buy pets with them in the WARDROBE.

Treasure chests

Grab chests (look for the light beam). At the end of the run, open the gift box: clothes, coins or diamonds!

Time pads (Time Rift)

Green pads speed time up for double points. Purple pads slow time down.

Infinite World ∞

Level 0 never ends. It keeps getting faster: how far can you go?
